# Acme Technologies — Employee Handbook (HR Policy v4.2)

Effective: 1 April 2026 | Owner: People Operations | Applies to: All full-time employees in India

## Leave Policy

New employees are entitled to 24 days of paid leave per calendar year: 16 days of earned leave, 8 days of casual/sick leave. Earned leave accrues at 1.33 days per month and unused earned leave up to 30 days carries forward to the next year. Casual and sick leave lapse on 31 December.

In addition, all employees receive 10 public holidays per year as per the regional holiday calendar published every January. Employees in their first year accrue leave proportionally from their date of joining.

Maternity leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave as per the Maternity Benefit Act. Paternity leave is 2 weeks of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth. Bereavement leave is 5 paid days for immediate family.

Leave requests must be raised in the HRMS portal at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave. Sick leave can be applied retroactively within 2 days of returning, with a medical certificate required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Probation and Confirmation

The probation period for all new hires is 6 months. Confirmation is automatic unless the reporting manager initiates an extension (maximum one extension of 3 months) at least 2 weeks before the probation end date. During probation, the notice period is 15 days; after confirmation it is 60 days.

## Work From Home Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval and are reviewed every 6 months. All remote work must be performed from within India for tax and data-compliance reasons unless an international remote work exception is approved by Legal and the CHRO.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST, Monday to Friday, with flexible start time between 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, during which employees are expected to be reachable.

## Code of Conduct Summary

Harassment of any kind results in disciplinary action up to termination. All complaints go to the Internal Committee (IC) at ic@acmetech.example and are acknowledged within 48 hours. Conflicts of interest, including moonlighting for competitors, must be disclosed to HR in writing.
