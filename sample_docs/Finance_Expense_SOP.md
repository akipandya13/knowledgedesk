# Acme Technologies — Expense & Procurement SOP (Finance v3.1)

Effective: 1 January 2026 | Owner: Finance Controller

## Expense Approval Matrix

- Up to ₹10,000: self-approved, submit receipts within 30 days.
- ₹10,001 to ₹50,000: reporting manager approval required.
- Above ₹50,000: requires Department Head approval AND Finance Controller sign-off. Expenses above ₹50,000 without prior written approval will not be reimbursed.
- Above ₹5,00,000 (capital expenditure): CFO approval plus a purchase order raised through the procurement portal before any vendor commitment.

## Travel Policy

Domestic flights: economy class only. Bookings must be made through the corporate travel desk at least 7 days in advance; exceptions need manager approval. Hotel cap: ₹6,000 per night in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Ahmedabad, Kolkata), ₹4,000 per night elsewhere.

Per diem for domestic travel is ₹1,500 per day covering meals and incidentals; no receipts required. International per diem is USD 60 per day. Local commute during business travel is reimbursed at actuals against receipts; ride-hailing apps preferred over private taxis.

## Equipment and Laptop Reimbursement

The laptop reimbursement limit is ₹1,20,000 including GST, refreshed every 3 years. Engineers in the ML/Data teams may request an enhanced limit of ₹1,80,000 with VP Engineering approval. Monitors, keyboards, and peripherals have a combined limit of ₹25,000 per refresh cycle.

Home office setup (one-time, for remote-approved employees): up to ₹15,000 against receipts for a desk and chair. Monthly internet reimbursement is ₹1,500, paid with salary against a declaration; no bills required.

## Reimbursement Timelines

Claims submitted and approved by the 20th of the month are paid with that month's salary. Claims older than 90 days from the expense date are rejected automatically by the system. Forex expenses are reimbursed at the RBI reference rate on the transaction date.

## Vendor Onboarding

New vendors require: GST certificate, cancelled cheque, signed MSA, and a vendor code issued by Finance (3 working days SLA). Payments follow net-30 terms by default; net-15 requires Finance Controller approval.
