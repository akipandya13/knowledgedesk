# Acme Technologies — Customer Refund & SLA Policy (CS v2.5)

Effective: 1 March 2026 | Owner: VP Customer Success | Classification: Internal

## Refund Policy by Customer Tier

Enterprise clients (annual contract value above ₹20,00,000): refunds are pro-rated for the unused contract period and require approval from the VP Customer Success. Enterprise clients may terminate for convenience with 90 days written notice; in that case the refund covers full unused months only, and the one-time implementation fee is non-refundable. If termination is due to an SLA breach by Acme (see Section: SLA Credits), the client receives a full pro-rated refund including the current month plus any accrued SLA credits.

Growth tier clients (₹5,00,000 to ₹20,00,000 ACV): 30-day money-back guarantee from the contract start date, no questions asked. After 30 days, refunds are pro-rated for full unused quarters and require Customer Success Director approval.

Starter tier and monthly subscriptions: no refunds for the current billing month; cancellation stops the next renewal. Exceptions for billing errors are processed within 7 working days.

All refunds are processed to the original payment method within 15 working days of approval. Refunds above ₹10,00,000 additionally require CFO sign-off.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Uptime commitment: 99.9% monthly uptime for Enterprise, 99.5% for Growth, measured excluding scheduled maintenance windows (announced 72 hours in advance, max 4 hours per month, scheduled 1:00–5:00 AM IST on Sundays).

SLA credits for Enterprise: uptime 99.0–99.9% earns a 5% credit of the monthly fee; 95.0–98.99% earns 15%; below 95% earns 30%. Credits are applied to the next invoice and must be claimed within 30 days of the incident month. Three consecutive months below 99% gives the client the right to terminate with a full pro-rated refund.

## Support Response Times

- Severity 1 (production down): first response within 30 minutes for Enterprise, 2 hours for Growth. 24x7 coverage.
- Severity 2 (major feature degraded): 4 business hours.
- Severity 3 (minor issue / question): 1 business day.

Escalation path: Support Engineer → Support Lead → VP Customer Success. Enterprise clients have a named Customer Success Manager and a quarterly business review.

## Data Retention After Termination

Customer data is retained for 60 days after contract end for export, then permanently deleted within 30 further days. A signed certificate of deletion is provided to Enterprise clients on request.
